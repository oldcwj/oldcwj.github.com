FIT File Viewer & Repair Support

Help and contact information for FIT File Viewer & Repair on iPhone and iPad.

About the app

FIT File Viewer & Repair helps you open FIT activity files on your device, review activity details and route information, diagnose file issues, repair supported files, and export data as GPX, CSV, or a repaired FIT file.

Getting started

  1. Save or locate the FIT file in the iOS Files app.
  2. Open the file with FIT File Viewer & Repair, or select it from within the app.
  3. Review the activity information, diagnostics, and Map view where available.
  4. Choose an export or repair option only when you are ready to save or share the result.

Your FIT file is processed locally on your device. The app does not upload it to Coobbi.

Common questions

Why can't I reopen a recent file?

The original file may have been moved, renamed, deleted, or become unavailable in its storage provider. Locate the file again in the Files app and open it with the app.

Why is there no route on the map?

Map view is available only when the FIT file contains route coordinates. FIT files without GPS location data will not display a route.

Where are exported files saved?

You choose the destination when you save or share an exported GPX, CSV, or repaired FIT file. Check the Files location or the app you selected in the iOS share sheet.

How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Use the app's purchase or restore option while signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple manages payment processing and purchase restoration.

Contact support

Please include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, a short description of the issue, and steps to reproduce it. Do not send private FIT files unless they are needed for troubleshooting and you are comfortable sharing them.
